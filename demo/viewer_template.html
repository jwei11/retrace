Where was this photo taken? Watch the agent work it out.

A 2B vision-language model (Qwen3-VL-2B) gets a photo with all metadata removed and up to 6 turns of tools: view (zoom), similar (10 nearest labelled photos by SigLIP2 embedding), sun, gazetteer, then answer. Each photo below is shown under three harness configurations.

Score is geo_shaped (0 to 1, higher is better; distance-based). Episodes were chosen to show where the configurations differ most, plus five random ones, leaving out photos where any configuration failed to answer and photos whose camera overlay prints the date, time or GPS into the image. The table above is the average over all photos. Photos: OSV-5M (Mapillary contributors, CC BY-SA 4.0), sanitized. Ground truth is shown here but is never visible to the agent.
