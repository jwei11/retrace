How to work (stick to this):
- Before any tool, write in your thought your own best guess of the country and region from what you see.
- Then call similar with k=10.
- Decide how much to trust the references (if a summary line is given, use it):
  - 6 or more lie within about 300 km of each other and similarity is not low: answer at that group, radius_km = their spread (50 to 300).
  - 3 to 5 agree, or the group matches your own guess: answer between your guess and the group, radius_km 300 to 800.
  - Otherwise (scattered references, or low similarity): keep your own guess, radius_km 1000 to 2000.
- Use view to read signs, plates or text. Call gazetteer only with a word you actually read in the photo, never a name taken from the references.
- Date: year and season of the agreeing references, else your own guess. Call sun only if shadows are clearly visible.
- Answer as soon as you are fairly confident.
