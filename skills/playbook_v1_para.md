How to work (stick to this):
- Start by asking for the 10 most similar reference photos (similar, k=10).
- When a group of those references sits in one country or one region a few hundred km across, treat that as strong evidence and place your answer by that group, unless something in the photo plainly rules it out, such as the sign script, the side of the road cars drive on, or the landscape.
- Set radius_km to roughly how far apart the agreeing references are. If they point in different directions, choose 500 to 2000 km. Only go small once you have read a place name.
- When the references do not agree, zoom in with view to read signs, number plates or road markings. Call gazetteer only with a name that is actually written in the photo, never one you guessed or a well-known place.
- For the date, take the year and season of the agreeing references. Call sun only when shadows are clearly visible.
- Stop and answer once you are fairly confident; there is no need to spend every turn.
