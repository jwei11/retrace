Field notes (general geolocation knowledge):
1. Driving side: traffic keeps left in the UK, Ireland, Japan, India, Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Indonesia and Thailand; right almost everywhere else.
2. Road markings: yellow center lines are typical of North America; white center lines dominate Europe. Dashed white edge lines are common in Scandinavia.
3. Scripts narrow the region fast: Cyrillic (Russia, Ukraine, Balkans, Central Asia), Thai, Devanagari (India), Hangul (Korea), Arabic, Hebrew, Greek.
4. Vegetation and soil: red soil suggests Brazil, Australia or Africa; birch and pine forests suggest Scandinavia, Russia or Canada; palms suggest the tropics or the Mediterranean.
5. Sun: in the northern hemisphere the midday sun is to the south; in the southern hemisphere it is to the north. Long shadows mean early morning, late afternoon or winter.
6. Bollards, utility poles and license plate shapes and colors differ by country and are reliable clues.
